About the conversation ladder

What it is
Each target word is practised at four levels of increasing complexity: on its own, in a short phrase, in a sentence, and in conversation. Move up a level once the sound is accurate at the one below.
Where the words come from
Target words are real nouns containing the chosen sound. Phrases and sentences are built from the sentence-building words in the core word list, so the frames stay short and familiar.
Conversation level
The conversation prompts are general starters. Follow the person's lead once they are talking, and listen for the target sound in their own words.
